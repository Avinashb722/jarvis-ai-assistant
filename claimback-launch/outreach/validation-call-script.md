# Validation Call Script — Days 1–3 (10 calls, $0 cost)

**Goal:** Confirm the pain exists in your market before building anything. Log every answer.

## Who to call
10 HVAC/plumbing shops, 5–50 techs, in your metro. Call mid-morning (9:30–11am) — after dispatch rush, before lunch. Ask for the **office manager** (they file claims and answer phones).

## The script

> "Hi, this is {{name}} — quick, slightly odd question, I'm not selling anything today. I'm researching how service shops handle **manufacturer warranty claims** — like when a tech swaps a compressor or a water heater that's still under parts warranty. Who handles filing those claims with Carrier or Rheem at your shop?"

*(Let them answer. Then:)*

> "Roughly how often does a claimable part get replaced but the claim never gets filed — like, is that rare, or does it happen a lot?"

*(Then the money question:)*

> "If someone found and filed all the ones that slip through — no charge unless money comes back — would that be interesting, or is it not enough money to matter?"

## What to log (per call)

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Company / size | # techs if they mention |
| Who files claims | Owner / office mgr / "the distributor" / nobody |
| Slippage estimate | Rare / sometimes / "all the time" |
| Reaction to offer | Cold / curious / "call me back" |
| Objection heard | verbatim |

## Kill / go signals
- **GO:** ≥4 of 10 say claims slip through regularly AND ≥3 show interest in the free audit.
- **PIVOT:** everyone says "our distributor handles it at the counter" → the wedge is *tracking + deadline enforcement*, not filing. Adjust pitch.
- **KILL:** nobody can estimate slippage and nobody cares → pain isn't felt. Re-run in plumbing/water heaters before killing entirely.

## Bonus close (if they're warm)
> "Tell you what — send me 90 days of invoices, I'll tell you the exact dollar figure sitting unfiled. Free, three days, NDA if you want. Worst case you learn your office manager is crushing it."
