# Pilot Offer + Authorization Template (Week 1)

## The pilot pitch (for the first 5 shops)

> "We're onboarding 5 founding shops this month. Instead of our normal
> 75/25 split, founding shops keep **85%** for the first 6 months, forever
> priority support, and we do a free registration-compliance sweep on your
> recent installs. In exchange: an honest testimonial if we deliver, and
> patience while we tune the process. No recovery, no fee, cancel anytime."

Why 85/15 instead of free: free pilots don't behave like customers and
their testimonials are worthless ("of course it was worth it, it was free").
15% keeps skin in the game both ways.

## Letter of Authorization (adapt with a lawyer before first use)

```
LETTER OF AUTHORIZATION — WARRANTY CLAIM FILING

{{Company Legal Name}} ("Contractor") authorizes ClaimBack LLC ("Agent")
to prepare and submit manufacturer and distributor warranty claims on
Contractor's behalf, using Contractor's existing dealer and distributor
accounts, solely for parts purchased or installed by Contractor.

Agent may: access designated portal accounts; communicate with
manufacturers/distributors regarding claim status; view job invoices and
equipment records provided by Contractor for claim preparation.

Agent may NOT: purchase on Contractor's accounts, modify account settings,
or access customer payment information.

Compensation: {{15/25}}% of warranty credits issued as a result of claims
filed by Agent, invoiced upon credit issuance. No other fees.
Either party may terminate with 30 days' notice; fees apply to claims
already filed.

Contractor: ____________  Date: ______
ClaimBack:  ____________  Date: ______
```

## Data intake spec (what we ask shops to send)

- 90 days of job invoices (ServiceTitan/Housecall Pro export, or QuickBooks
  invoice CSV) — need: date, job description, parts lines, part numbers
- Equipment install records if available (or we work from invoice history)
- Distributor statements (Ferguson/etc.) — shows parts purchases + credits
- **Read-only. NDA on request. Never customer payment data.**

## Audit deliverable (the sales weapon)

One page, emailed as PDF:
1. **Headline number:** "Estimated unfiled claims, last 90 days: $X,XXX"
2. Table: date · part · brand · est. credit · **files by / EXPIRED** ← the deadline column does the closing
3. Annualized projection
4. "Here's what we'd file this week if you say go."
